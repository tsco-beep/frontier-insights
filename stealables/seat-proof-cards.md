# Frontier Field Guide  
**4 Seat‑Aware Proof Cards**  
Quick, actionable proof frameworks for Builders, CROs, Sales VPs, and Boards. One page per seat, ready to copy.

---

## Builder  
**Who it’s for:** Founder / Product Lead  

| What “proof” means | Inspectable signals | What NOT to show |
|---------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Show the product solves a real problem and has traction | 1️⃣ User adoption rate<br>2️⃣ Feature‑usage depth<br>3️⃣ Customer testimonials<br>4️⃣ Revenue growth<br>5️⃣ Road‑map alignment | 1️⃣ Buzzwords<br>2️⃣ Unverified claims<br>3️⃣ Internal‑only metrics |

**Monday ask**  
> “What single metric proves our product is indispensable to users?”

**Monday motion** (≤3 steps)  
1. Identify the key metric.  
2. Pull the latest data.  
3. Share the result with the team.

---

## CRO  
**Who it’s for:** Chief Revenue Officer  

| What “proof” means | Inspectable signals | What NOT to show |
|---------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Evidence that the revenue pipeline is healthy and scalable | 1️⃣ Pipeline velocity<br>2️⃣ Win rate<br>3️⃣ Avg. deal size<br>4️⃣ Forecast accuracy<br>5️⃣ Churn rate | 1️⃣ Unverified pipeline numbers<br>2️⃣ Anecdotal win stories<br>3️⃣ Internal forecasts only |

**Monday ask**  
> “Which pipeline metric is lagging behind target and why?”

**Monday motion** (≤3 steps)  
1. Pull the latest pipeline report.  
2. Highlight the gaps.  
3. Propose corrective action.

---

## Sales VP  
**Who it’s for:** VP of Sales  

| What “proof” means | Inspectable signals | What NOT to show |
|---------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Evidence that the sales team is executing and closing | 1️⃣ Quota attainment<br>2️⃣ Lead‑to‑close ratio<br>3️⃣ Sales‑cycle length<br>4️⃣ Activity metrics (calls, demos)<br>5️⃣ Pipeline health | 1️⃣ Raw activity without context<br>2️⃣ Unverified leads<br>3️⃣ Anecdotal success stories |

**Monday ask**  
> “What is our top blocker preventing quota attainment?”

**Monday motion** (≤3 steps)  
1. Review the quota dashboard.  
2. Identify blockers.  
3. Assign owners.

---

## Board  
**Who it’s for:** Board of Directors  

| What “proof” means | Inspectable signals | What NOT to show |
|---------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Evidence that the company is on track for growth and risk is managed | 1️⃣ Cash runway & burn<br>2️⃣ Market traction (ARR, growth)<br>3️⃣ Product‑market fit evidence<br>4️⃣ Governance & compliance<br>5️⃣ Risk‑mitigation plans | 1️⃣ Internal memos<br>2️⃣ Unverified forecasts<br>3️⃣ Incomplete risk assessments |

**Monday ask**  
> “What is the biggest risk to our next funding round?”

**Monday motion** (≤3 steps)  
1. Summarize the risk.  
2. Outline mitigation.  
3. Present to the board.
