# 30‑60‑90 Enablement Lead Playbook  
**A concise, operating‑system‑first roadmap for solo enablement leaders in pre‑IPO teams. Build systems, not content, and protect your calendar.**

---

## Who it’s for  
**Solo Enablement Lead** at a pre‑IPO, tiny‑team company (1‑3 people).  
You’re the only person pulling the enablement engine together—no content factory, just a lean operating system.

---

## 30‑60‑90 Day Field Guide

| Phase | Objectives | Outcomes | Calendar Protections | What to Refuse | Key Actions |
|-------|------------|----------|----------------------|----------------|-------------|
| **30 days** | 1. Map the current state<br>2. Identify quick‑wins | • Baseline inventory of tools, docs, and gaps<br>• 3 quick‑win system tweaks | • 1 h “Discovery” block every Monday<br>• 30 min “Status” check‑in with CEO | • “Content‑heavy” workshops<br>• Unplanned ad‑hoc requests | • Conduct a 1‑hour “State‑of‑Enablement” walk‑through with each team member<br>• Create a shared “Enablement Map” (tool‑list + workflow diagram)<br>• Draft a 30‑day “System Sprint” backlog |
| **60 days** | 1. Build core operating system<br>2. Automate repetitive tasks | • 2 core systems in place (e.g., onboarding pipeline, knowledge‑base workflow)<br>• 20 % reduction in manual hand‑offs | • 2 h “System Design” block every Tuesday<br>• 15 min “Refine” review each Friday | • “Show‑and‑tell” demos without impact<br>• “All‑hands” meetings that don’t move the needle | • Design and launch the onboarding pipeline (templates, checklists, automation triggers)<br>• Implement a knowledge‑base workflow (tagging, search, review cadence)<br>• Set up a weekly “System Health” dashboard |
| **90 days** | 1. Scale systems<br>2. Embed culture | • Systems adopted by 100 % of team<br>• Enablement metrics (time‑to‑competence, task‑completion) in place | • 1 h “Scale” block every Thursday<br>• 30 min “Celebrate” session each month | • “Feature‑first” mindset<br>• “One‑off” fixes that break the system | • Roll out system‑wide adoption training<br>• Capture and iterate on feedback loop (surveys, metrics)<br>• Publish a “Enablement Playbook” (living doc) for future hires |

> **Calendar Protection Principle**  
> Every week, reserve a *non‑negotiable* block for system work. Treat it like a board meeting—no one can book over it unless it’s a system‑critical emergency.

---

## Monday Motion (≤ 3 steps)

1. **Review the backlog** – Open the system backlog, highlight top 3 items that will move the operating system forward.  
2. **Prioritize** – Rank them by impact vs. effort; lock the top 3 into the week’s calendar.  
3. **Sync** – Send a 15‑min “Kick‑off” to the relevant stakeholder (product, engineering, HR) to confirm scope and ownership.
