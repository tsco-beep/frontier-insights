# Binary Deal‑Check / Stage‑Exit Checklist  
**Yes/no inspection for every stage. Keep deals on track. Exit when the signal is clear.**

---

## Who it's for  
Tiny enablement teams (1–3 people) running pipeline hygiene and stage gating in pre-IPO orgs.

---

### Stage‑Exit Rule  
- **Any ❌ No = flag for exit.**  
- Log reason in Notes. Move deal to *Exit Queue* for manager review.

### Daily Ritual (5 min)  
1. **Scan** for ❌ No marks.  
2. **Flag** deal in CRM.  
3. **Log** exit reason or next step in Notes.

---

## Monday Motion (3 Steps)  

1. **Pull** open deals in CRM.  
2. **Run** binary check on each stage.  
3. **Flag** exits; ping manager if pattern emerges.
