# Correction Loop Ritual  
**Challenge This Page** – Keep your field‑guide pages fresh and accurate.  
A quick, weekly ritual for 1‑3 enablement teammates.

---

## Who it’s for  
- Enablement Leads  
- Enablement Managers  
- Enablement Ops

---

## Ritual Checklist

| Step | Action | Owner | Frequency | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|-----------|-------|
| **1️⃣ Flag** | Scan the page for stale claims, broken links, or outdated data. | 1–3 team members | Weekly | Use the “Stale Claim” flag in the page’s comment section. |
| **2️⃣ Verify** | Cross‑check flagged items against the latest source (product docs, data dashboards, or stakeholder input). | 1–3 team members | Weekly | If you’re unsure, ping the relevant product owner. |
| **3️⃣ Patch** | Update the content, replace broken links, or add new data. Commit the change and add a brief note in the revision history. | 1–3 team members | Weekly | Keep the patch concise—focus on the specific claim, not the whole page. |
| **4️⃣ Review** | Quick peer‑review of the patch to catch typos or formatting drift. | 1–3 team members | Weekly | One person does the review; the other two approve. |

> **Operator Edge Tip:**  
> Keep the ritual lightweight—no more than 15 minutes per page. Use a shared spreadsheet or a simple Kanban board to track flagged pages.

---

## Monday Motion (≤3 steps)

1. **Pull the “Stale Claim” list** from the shared board.  
2. **Verify** each item against the latest source.  
3. **Patch** the page and close the card.
