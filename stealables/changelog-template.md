# Living Blueprint  
**What Got Sharper Changelog Template**  
*Capture the sharpest changes, why they mattered, and what to stop doing—ready for instant publish.*

---

## Who it’s for  
- Product Ops & Enablement Leads  
- Growth & Ops Managers  
- Anyone who needs a concise, actionable changelog

---

## Artifact Body – Copy‑Ready Template

| Date | What Got Sharper | Why It Mattered for the Field | What to Stop Doing | Source Claim Link |
|------|------------------|------------------------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **YYYY‑MM‑DD** | *Describe the sharpened feature, process, or insight* | *Explain the impact on users, revenue, or efficiency* | *List one or two practices that should be abandoned* | *URL to supporting data or documentation* |

> **Tip:** Keep each entry to 1‑2 sentences. Focus on the *action* and *outcome*.

---

## Ritual – Weekly Workflow

1. **Friday** – Gather the latest sharpenings from your team.  
2. **Friday** – Fill out the table above and review for clarity.  
3. **Friday** – Publish to the shared channel or newsletter.

---

## Monday Motion (≤ 3 steps)

1. Pull the template into your notes.  
2. Draft the new sharpenings from last week’s work.  
3. Publish and tag the relevant stakeholders.
