# Enablement Maturity Roadmap  
**1–5 Levels for Solo Leads**

A checklist to see where you are and what's next. No corporate fluff — just what moves the needle when you're a team of one.

---

## Who it's for  
Solo Enablement Leads at pre-IPO companies (1–3 person enablement teams) who need a clear path to scale without adding headcount first.

---

## Maturity Levels Overview  

| Level | What You're Building | Artifacts That Prove It | Quick Win | Gate to Next Level |
|-------|---------------------|------------------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **1 – Foundations** | Core onboarding & first playbook | • 30-day onboarding checklist<br>• Rep certification criteria (role-specific)<br>• First customer pitch template | Record 1 demo walkthrough that new hires can watch async | Manager can certify new reps in < 5 days (EXAMPLE) |
| **2 – Process & Tools** | Content library & performance visibility | • Searchable Notion/Guru library<br>• Weekly pipeline dashboard (by stage)<br>• Sales stage exit criteria | Tag & organize 10 most-used assets by deal stage | Sales can find the right asset in < 2 min (EXAMPLE) |
| **3 – Scale & Automation** | Self-serve learning paths & triggers | • Role-based onboarding sequences<br>• Auto-reminders for stalled deals<br>• Manager coaching checklist | Launch 1 Slack workflow that nudges reps when deals age > 14 days (EXAMPLE) | Onboarding runs without you scheduling every session |
| **4 – Data-Driven Refinement** | Leading indicators & iteration cycles | • Quarterly enablement scorecard (% certified, time-to-first-deal)<br>• Win/loss tied to training completion<br>• Content usage heatmap | Publish 1 scorecard that ties training to quota attainment | Leadership references your scorecard in QBRs |
| **5 – Continuous Culture** | Peer learning & manager ownership | • Monthly peer case-study sessions<br>• Manager-led role-play cadence<br>• Enablement council (cross-functional) | Launch 1 Slack channel where reps share "how I closed X" stories | Managers run >50% of skill sessions (EXAMPLE); you orchestrate, not deliver |

> **NOTE:** Timeframes, percentages, and thresholds (EXAMPLE) are illustrative. Calibrate to your ICP and sales cycle.

---

## Quick-Start Checklist (Ready to Copy)

**Monday morning, 15 minutes:**

- [ ] Mark the level that matches your current state (be honest — partial counts as the lower level)  
- [ ] Pick *one* Quick Win from your current level  
- [ ] Block 90 min this week to ship it  
- [ ] Add a Slack reminder to check next Monday: "Did the Quick Win land? What broke?"

**If you're between levels:** default to the lower one. Better to over-deliver on foundations than fake scale.

---

## Monday Motion (3 Steps)

1. **Morning audit** – Open the roadmap. Confirm your level. Write down the one Quick Win you're shipping this week.  
2. **Mid-week execution block** – 90 min, no Slack. Ship the Quick Win or get it 80% done.  
3. **Friday close** – Did it land? Update the roadmap. If blocked, note why and pick a smaller Quick Win for next Monday.
