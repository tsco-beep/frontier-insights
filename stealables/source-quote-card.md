# Quotable Source Card  
**Capture, verify, and share insights in one place.**  
Use this quick template to surface evidence that drives decisions—no fluff, just facts.

---

## Who it’s for  
- Product & Growth Leads  
- Enablement & Ops teams  
- Anyone who needs a single source of truth for insights

---

## Artifact Body – Ready to Copy  

| Claim | Quote | Source URL | Seat | Why it Matters | Verified? |
|-------|-------|------------|------|----------------|-----------|
| `INSERT CLAIM` | `INSERT QUOTE` | `https://example.com` | `INSERT SEAT` | `INSERT WHY IT MATTERS` | `Yes / No` |

**Checklist**  
- [ ] Capture the claim in one sentence.  
- [ ] Pull the exact quote that supports the claim.  
- [ ] Add the URL (or file path) to the source.  
- [ ] Tag the seat (role that owns the insight).  
- [ ] Explain why the insight matters in 1‑2 lines.  
- [ ] Mark as *Verified* once the source is confirmed.

---

## Monday Motion (≤ 3 steps)

1. **Spot** an insight during a meeting or review.  
2. **Fill** the card in the shared repo.  
3. **Verify** the source with the relevant stakeholder.
